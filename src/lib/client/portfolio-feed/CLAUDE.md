# Portfolio

The Portfolio section of `/misc` (`Portfolio.astro`, `portfolio.ts`, this directory, styles in `src/styles/portfolio/`). `../CLAUDE.md` covers what every live section shares.

- **Portfolio** (`Portfolio.astro`, `portfolio.ts`, `portfolio-feed/`): the portfolio as the owner keeps it twice — the money from Wealthfolio, the securities (profiles, closes) from Ghostfolio — as **returns, never amounts** — per account its value and the money put in as ratios on one scale (`value`, `invested`), holdings by weight plus cash's weight, daily closes of every holding and of VOO (labelled S&P 500), and per account its best days (`best`: newest first, each beating every day after it, read off the worker's daily returns; `bestDay()` takes the earliest dated after a range's start, so ALL is answered daily though its axis is weekly). The hero figure is the range's return, muted when it is a fall like every down figure — **what the money did** (`moneyReturns()`: the range's gain over the value it started from plus everything put in since, so a deposit never reads as a gain) — with only the range under it (no "All accounts" over the whole portfolio's deck); the readout is what it hides — Last session, vs S&P 500 (the same money put into it on the same days, its return in the note, two decimals), Max drawdown, Best day; the session, drawdown and strip read the account's `growth`, the worker's daily returns chained (money moving never moves it, and a day starting from dust earns nothing — a rule the ratios cannot carry, so the page never re-derives it; `growth()` does so only for `/now`'s tile), and a range starting on the weekly part of the axis takes its drawdown from the worker's daily `falls` (`rangeDrawdown()`), since a week's peak and trough are not on the axis. The axis is US market sessions (daily for the last year, weekly before, keeping every day money moved and the day before it so a range's returns stay the daily ones), and the ranges (1W 1M 3M YTD 1Y ALL) are the page's: `availableRanges()` drops one that would reach past the account's start and so repeat ALL. One ink line (the range's return day by day, as levels from 1) with the same money in the benchmark as a dashed ghost, keyed in a row over the chart (`.chart-key`, the home tile's too) — never a label inside the plot, where it sat on its own line; the strip under it is the daily returns in day/week/month cells (`grainFor`), gains in the heat greens and losses in ink (`--down-1…4`) — **no red anywhere**, the site keeps one colour, and a down figure is muted with a true minus. The chart is a `role="slider"` (arrows, Home/End) whose scrubber drives the hero figure and lights its strip cell. Accounts (a segmented control with each broker's mark) and ranges share one toolbar above the hero. The holdings are a table by weight (`view()`): ticker and full name in Inter, the range's sparkline, latest price, **average cost per share** (the worker's `cost`; the only per-position money figure, and a price, never an amount), weight and the **position's own return** (Wealthfolio's total return — gain, realised, unrealised and income, over what went into it) — the sparkline and the return each coloured by its own sign. The table reads its own width (`container-type`): the sparkline goes first, then the cost folds under the price. A row opens in place (`fillPanel`, facts from `holdingFacts()`): the price with its move as a tinted figure, then sector and country as outlined tags and the site with an external arrow on the line under them, the price line against an unlabelled dashed cost level with the gap washed green above and grey below and the first buy dotted, its range tabs with the key to both marks (cost, first buy) on the same row, a four-cell readout (avg cost, vs cost, last session, held) and the 52-week band with price and cost on it; it also outlines its allocation tile (a hovered row too). **Allocation** sits between the readout and the table: a squarified treemap (`treemap.ts`, d3's squarify kept in-house, tested) of the account's holdings, area by weight, each tile graded by the last session's move or the position's return (`TILE_STEPS`, a Day / Return segmented control like the accounts') in the strip's greens and inks, cash hatched and printing no figure; `allocLayout()` lays the cash-like block (cash plus `PORTFOLIO.cashLike` in `consts.ts`, BOXX today) and the invested one as one rectangle each, and the map **opens zoomed into the invested block** (the heading reads `Allocation / Invested 13.7%`, the word dropped below 30rem). An expand button beside Day / Return (a square of the segmented track; its four corners turn half a turn each to point in when pressed) widens it to the whole account over 250ms — the cash-like tiles ride the same zoom back into the frame — and restores it (Escape too); hovering it, or keyboard focus, on the whole account rings the invested block and steps the rest back. Hovering a tile shows a card with its logo or flag, name, weight, last session and return (`tileCard()`); clicking a holding's tile opens its row in the table and scrolls it into view (a tap shows the card first). The map's height is the column's (`cqi`), never its content's (`allocation.ts` is the runtime). Cash is a row per currency marked with its flag (`CASH_FLAGS`, square PNGs in `src/assets/flags/`; a currency without one gets a banknote). Logos are the Worker's mirrored logo.dev copies in rounded-square tiles on white, a monogram for one that fails. The brokers' marks (`account.logo`) sit over the hero figure as a small overlapping deck (`fillKicker`); picking an account folds the others away, and the tiles persist across draws so that animates.
